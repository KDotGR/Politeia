## Full description

INDEPENDENT APP — NOT A GOVERNMENT SERVICE
Politeia is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of the Greek government, the Ministry of Interior, or any government entity.

Politeia is a bilingual Greek and English election calculator for exploring how votes become seats.

Choose a Parliament, European Parliament, local or custom election. Enter party percentages for Parliament and European Parliament elections, or percentages or vote counts for local and custom elections, and receive a seat allocation with an explanation of every step. Parliament scenarios also include a coalition simulator for testing whether selected parties reach the 151-seat majority target.

The app includes dated historical election and local-election reference datasets, configurable custom-election rules, party colours, responsive navigation, optional sounds and links to the sources used for published data. Historical scenarios are reference simulations; they are not an official election result, prediction or government service.

All calculations are unofficial estimates and scenario simulations. Poll data comes from third-party providers and is not government election data or an official forecast. For official election results and electoral legislation, consult the original government sources:

Greek Ministry of Interior — official election results:
https://ekloges.ypes.gr/

Greek Ministry of Interior — electoral legislation:
https://www.ypes.gr/eklogiki-nomothesia/

Third-party poll source — PolitPro:
https://politpro.eu/en/greece

No account is required. Scenarios and preferences remain on the device. Optional poll-source refresh uses published web sources and may be disabled by the user.
