# Politeia privacy policy

**Effective date:** 11 September 2026

Privacy questions can be raised through the [Politeia GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/KDotGR/Politeia/issues).

Politeia calculates illustrative election seat allocations and coalition scenarios. It is not an official government service, election authority, or prediction service.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores language, sound preference, election settings, party inputs, and saved scenarios locally on the device. Politeia does not receive these values and has no account or sign-in.

## Network requests

The app may open published source links in the browser and may refresh the optional poll-source catalogue. These requests may expose ordinary technical metadata to the relevant source provider. Politeia does not intentionally collect or share personal data and includes no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Background refresh

If enabled, Android may run a background refresh service for poll information. Background activity can be controlled through Android settings and the app settings.

## Third-party services and changes

External source providers have their own privacy policies. This policy may be updated when the app changes; the GitHub issue tracker is the contact channel for privacy questions.
