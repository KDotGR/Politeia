Politeia / Πολιτεία privacy policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

Privacy questions can be raised through the Politeia GitHub issue tracker.

What the app does

Politeia calculates illustrative election seat allocations and coalition scenarios. It is not an official government service, an election authority, or a prediction service.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the language, sound preference, selected election settings, party inputs and saved scenarios locally on your device. We do not receive these values, and there is no account or sign-in.

Network requests

The app may open published source links in your browser and may refresh the optional poll-source catalogue. These requests use the network and may expose ordinary technical metadata to the relevant source provider. Politeia does not intentionally collect or share personal data, and it does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Background refresh

If enabled by the app, Android may run a background refresh service for poll information. You can control background activity through Android settings and the app's settings.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Third-party services

When you follow an external source link, the destination provider's privacy policy applies. This policy does not govern those providers.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. For privacy questions, use the GitHub issue tracker linked above.
